# The 20 designs added in September 2026: what they're based on

The eight original designs (Sahar Bordeaux, Golden Papercut, Rooftop at Dusk, Ramon at Dusk, Caesarea
Shore, Honey Meadow, Nitzan, Atara) are photographic or watercolour, mostly for weddings and family
events. The twenty new ones were chosen to cover what that set didn't — other visual languages and other
events — after surveying what digital-invitation and stationery sites offer and what's trending:

- **Graphic styles people pick most for e-invitations:** florals (light and dark), minimal/"quiet
  luxury", art deco, vintage/retro, hand-drawn, illustration for kids, and cultural patterns.
- **Trends of 2024–2026 in invitations and party design:** dark-and-moody florals, the "coquette"
  bow-and-pearl look, neon, retro cocktail graphics, travel/passport stationery, "imperfect" hand-drawn
  collages, Mediterranean tiles and lemons.
- **Local, Israeli and Jewish subjects** no generic site has: Jerusalem stone, Tel Aviv's Bauhaus, the
  red anemone and the almond bloom, the Torah scroll for a bar/bat mitzvah, a Moroccan and a Bukharan
  henna.
- **Events that had little choice:** henna, corporate events, kids' birthdays, brit, baby showers,
  bar/bat mitzvahs with a theme (football, neon).

Every design is drawn in code (an SVG scene, not a photo), so it loads fast, stays sharp on any screen,
and takes the colour presets and the font pairs like the others. Ten are premium.
